# Nuvio + IPTV Mobile 1.1.0-beta.2-iptv.2

## What's new

### Sports
- **A new Sports hub.** A search bar, a big swipe card, **Live on your channels**, leagues as folding groups with the next match day and a short table, and game cards in team colours. My Sports shows each team's place and form
- **The game page** has a row of the day's games, a scoreboard, a Watch button and a line under every channel that says if it's showing the game now or later. After the final whistle the replays come first
- **More highlights.** Races, fights, golf, tennis, darts and more get highlights and full replays from the sports' own YouTube channels. Older games and past races keep theirs, and race, fight and tournament rows go back through the whole season
- **Show Highlights** and **Show Condensed Games** switches in Settings → Sports
- **Handball** added. College tables have conference tabs and real records
- Followed teams show results from every competition (Nations League, friendlies, cups), and league rows keep the last week's results

### Playback
- **Backups button** over a playing channel lists the other channels showing the same game, then your sports add-ons' streams, then the same channel from your other playlists
- **Best quality:** a new auto-play option that picks the best source. Edit the **Quality rules**, or sort sources with **Best quality first** (Settings → Playback → Stream Auto-play)
- **Seek thumbnails** fill in much sooner
- **Closed captions** show inside the video on Live TV

### Recordings
- **Your TVs' recordings show on your phone.** Recordings saved to a network folder (NAS or shared folder) appear in Recordings and play from there, even while a TV is still recording. Delete one and it's gone for every TV
- **Set up the network folder on your phone** (Live TV → Recordings → Network folder), with **Find on Network** to look for your NAS or PC, or let it come over with Setup Sync. It works with the VPN in Full app mode too
- **Recorded, Scheduled and Series tabs** with posters, and a gear in the header for Recordings settings
- **Fix Match:** long-press a recording or an On Demand title and search for the right show or movie, or paste an IMDb ID or TMDB link. A pick covers every recording of that show and syncs with your TVs

### Home
- **Live TV, Sports and On Demand rows on Home.** Add a row for Favorites, a playlist group, games on now, a movie or series category, Recently added or Continue Watching On Demand. Rows you made on your TV show here too
- **On Demand** has a new **Hide Non-English Titles** switch, and titles with tags like "[4K]" or "(EN Subs)" are cleaned up and matched better

### Live TV
- **Live TV starts instantly.** Your channel list shows straight away when you open the app, and only changed playlists are read again
- **Favorite groups:** hold a group to star it. Starred groups sit at the top
- **Show on Profiles** for each playlist
- **Lock Playlist Logins** (under Parental Controls): when it's on, editing playlists and guides and the network folder ask for your PIN
- A **User agent** box for guides, and a switch to leave movies and series out of M3U playlists
- Episode names show beside the title in the guide and in show details

## Fixed
- **Multiview** panes reconnect when Android briefly takes the video away, buffering is smoother, and the loading spinner only shows while a pane is waiting for picture
- **Race games** like MotoGP now get their channels, and **NFL game tables** show both teams' divisions in full
- Only real wrestling shows appear in Live & Upcoming
- Rugby shows the right match minute
- Sports channels you search for are named right away for every game
- Shows marked new no longer show the mark twice
- A broken guide file no longer replaces a better saved guide, and a full storage shows a clear message
- **Back** in the guide closes the search and returns you to your group
